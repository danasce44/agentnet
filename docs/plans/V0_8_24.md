## Integrated release corrections

The new ordinary-agent send reused the existing encrypted transport, but had
three uncovered paths: group receive guards conflated a human guest audience
with execution, browser history parsing excluded proactive named messages,
and archive descriptors only declared format support. Corrections keep the exact
`agent:proactive` shape, current signed agent/host/topic/audience authority and
independent per-reader gates. They add no service, schema, protocol field or
execution permission.

Archive packing persists `ams1` in the existing required-capability column and
checks `ha1` separately. Rootless descriptors park in Waiting until a signed
reader update. Repacking expired archives inherits the previous descriptor's
requirement even after local child plaintext is erased. The encrypted child
and descriptor bytes stay unchanged across ordinary retry. Inert linked group
history can retain a previously accepted, cleanly dismissed agent's proactive
messages; the live admission gate still refuses new traffic after dismissal.

A separate race failure came from `TestDMAgentOldAnsweredStatusRecovery`
starting the phone daemon twice: `linkedVia` already starts it. The duplicate
start is removed without changing daemon behavior or weakening assertions.
