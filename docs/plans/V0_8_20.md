## Recipient lifecycle contract

- Permanent relay revocation ends undelivered copies addressed to exact
  revoked keys, using existing terminal receipts; it never invents delivery.
  Owner decision (2026-10-11): previously accepted messages still arrive after
  normal phone replacement or offboarding. Preserve ordinary message/answer/
  result custody from the removed sender; expire its unadmitted execution and
  background sync. Clients allow ordinary content only with retained relay
  ingress provenance and normal signature/scope checks, never direct sends or
  authority-bearing controls. Do not trust sender timestamps for this boundary.
  Whole-person email denial covers enrolled and pending devices, even when
  the email domain remains allowed. New requests and open streams are refused.
  `admin revoke --person ID` also handles non-Google persons atomically;
  `person list` exposes locally known exact IDs and verification state. Its
  response prints exact revoked addresses; independently enrolled standalone
  devices without a verified person link require explicit address revocation.
- Device roster removal revokes link-enrolled devices. An independently
  enrolled device detached from its person remains standalone; end only its
  own-person synchronization authority, not unrelated ordinary conversations.
- Group membership removal affects that conversation's existing admission
  rules, not unrelated workspace access. The encrypted relay cannot invent
  conversation authority or erase plaintext already delivered to a member.
- Offline and outdated devices remain resumable. Their copies must not block
  other recipients or create repeated active work; absence is not revocation.
- Retain original messages, files, delivered evidence and other recipients'
  copies. Replacement enrollment uses a new key and current retained history,
  never retargets ciphertext or migrates execution authority.
- Revocation races must preserve delivered-first outcomes and reject new
  admission after revocation. Recovery of existing stranded copies must be
  idempotent across restart. Queued local/archive work must also settle.
- Transport expiry is not execution completion or cancellation. Known revoked
  senders must not start new requests; preserve completed history and do not
  rerun tasks. Offline peers cannot learn revocation before reconnecting.
